{{- /* Answers one question: should search engines leave this page out of their index? True pages get (see header.html) and are dropped from the sitemap (see layouts/sitemap.xml). The page itself keeps rendering, so the URL, its backlinks and aliases stay alive. A post opts in with `noindex: true` in its front matter. A taxonomy term page (a series or a tag) has no front matter of its own, so it follows its posts: it is left out when every post in it is left out. Add one indexable post to such a series and the series page comes back by itself. */ -}} {{- $noindex := .Params.noindex | default false -}} {{- if eq .Kind "term" -}} {{- $noindex = or $noindex (eq (len (where .Pages "Params.noindex" "ne" true)) 0) -}} {{- end -}} {{- return $noindex -}}
