Discover got a lot more useful — read what people think before you watch, save titles for later, see what's coming next, and keep a cleaner feed.

## Reviews Before You Watch

Open any movie or show on Discover and you'll see a new **Reviews** tab right next to Episodes and Streams. It pulls in what real viewers thought — so you can decide whether something's worth your evening before you even start it.

Long plot descriptions don't get cut off anymore either. When a description is trimmed, just tap the **"…"** to expand it inline, and **"↑"** to collapse it back.

## Save Titles to Your Watchlist

Spotted something for later? Tap the **heart** on any card — in the catalog, search, AI suggestions, or the details popup — and it lands in your **Watchlist**. A Catalog / Watchlist switcher at the top takes you straight to everything you've saved.

Saving also makes your recommendations sharper: the titles you keep tell the AI what you're into, so its suggestions land closer to your taste. Free accounts can save up to 200 titles; paid accounts are unlimited.

## An Episode Calendar for Your Shows

Following a series? There's a new **calendar view** that lays out upcoming episodes on a timeline instead of a wall of posters. You can see what's airing over the next few weeks at a glance.

When a show drops a whole season at once, it collapses into a single "Season 3 · 8 episodes" card so your timeline stays clean. Tap an episode to jump straight to streams.

## Discover Now Speaks Your Language

Movie titles and descriptions on Discover used to show up in English no matter what language you were using. Now they appear **translated in your language** — across all 11 languages Webtor supports. Browsing finally feels native.

## Share Right From the Player

There's now a **share button built into the video player**, so you can send a link without leaving what you're watching. And when you paste that link somewhere, it shows a proper preview — poster, title, and a frame from the video — instead of a bare URL.

## Faster Start

Videos start quicker now. And if you open a link someone just shared with you, the start is almost instant — when the content is already loaded, playback begins right away instead of making you wait.

## NSFW Hidden by Default

**NSFW content on Discover is now hidden behind a blur and an 18+ badge by default.** It won't show up in your feed unless you ask for it — so Discover stays safe to open in public or around others.

If you want it shown, turn it on in your settings, or reveal a single card with a tap. Off by default, fully your call.

## Improved Stability

A handful of behind-the-scenes fixes you won't see but will feel — smoother handling of rare torrents, fewer hiccups when sharing links, and quicker recovery when something restarts in the background.

## Coming Next: Release Notifications

We're working on subscriptions for releases — so you'll get a heads-up when a movie or show you're waiting for finally comes out, or when something you follow gets a new release or update. No more checking back manually.
